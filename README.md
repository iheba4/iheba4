## Iheb Aissa

Final year engineering student in Automation and Control at the École Nationale Polytechnique de Constantine, Algeria.

I got into this because I wanted to make robots move. I taught myself Python as a teenager for exactly that reason, and most of what I build still ends with something physical reacting to a control loop: a drone holding attitude in a gust, a rocket deciding when to open its airbrakes, a camera that tracks a face through a full turn.

These days my work falls into three buckets. Flight software for a student sounding rocket, where I am the solo automation lead. Real time perception that has to run on edge hardware instead of a workstation. And control theory, mostly nonlinear and adaptive, because that is what keeps the first two honest once reality stops cooperating.

### Tools I reach for

Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, scikit learn. C and C++ on ESP32, Arduino and Raspberry Pi. MATLAB and Simulink for control design. Zybo FPGA with Vivado. Siemens S7 with TIA Portal and WinCC on the industrial side. LaTeX for anything that has to be read.

### Background

Ranked 3rd in my class with a 14.24 average. Placed 40th out of 844 nationally at the École Polytechnique d'Alger entrance exam in 2024, then stayed at ENPC to finish the degree.

I spent a research immersion at CRTI Chéraga on embedded AI and computer vision, building two autonomous mobile robots that pair a live vision pipeline with real time PID motor control. Before that, two industrial internships: FERTIAL, a 550 kt/yr fertilizer plant running a Yokogawa CENTUM DCS with 2oo3 safety voting, and Briqueterie El-Amel, a fully automated brick line on Siemens S7-300 and S7-400 with a 15 zone kiln.

I cofounded the SkyBridge Club at ENPC and was elected President in February 2026. We run rocketry, robotics and workshops, and I coordinated the AEC Engineering Hackathon 2026 for eastern Algeria.
